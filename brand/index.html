Rules of use

  • Clear space. Keep one hub-diameter of empty space on every side. Nothing crowds the seedhead.
  • Do not recolour the pins outside the three-colour set, rotate the mark, add a stem or leaf, or place the full mark on a busy photograph.
  • The two red pins are the point. One head, a hundred landings. They never get cropped off — if the mark does not fit, use compact rather than clipping the drift.
  • Small sizes. Compact to 24px. Glyph below that. Mark C for thread.
  • The weed question. A dealer will say it out loud eventually. The answer is one sentence and every rep should have the same one: one head, a hundred landings, and it grows anywhere.
