Standing rules

What never changes, whatever tool you are in

  • Wellbeing before business. Every member contact opens with “Are you safe? Are you in a secure location? Is everything okay?” Never with billing, never with the account.
  • No member information anywhere in these tools. No names, addresses, account numbers, or panel account codes. Identity lives in the CRM.
  • Dealers by DLR number only in anything shared.
  • Code citations carry the standard, the edition year, and the section — and the AHJ has final say on all of it.
  • Licensing routes to Compliance. Contract language routes to legal review. You do not answer either one in the field.
  • Permit, monitoring, and false-alarm ordinance deviations get flagged immediately, mid-job if necessary.
  • Price comes from the price authority. Component roll-ups are not sell prices. Unverified means you do not say it.
  • Canvas → Convert → Cultivate. That wording, every time.
